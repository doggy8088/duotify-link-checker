## 01-modular-link：幾何拼接

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original Chrome extension icon concept for Duotify Link Checker, a tool that checks website links.
Primary request: a new distinctive and polished icon, suitable for both a 16-pixel browser toolbar and a 128-pixel extension listing.
Composition/framing: square canvas, ONE centered compact icon with about 6% safe margin. Main strokes and openings are broad; the main silhouette must survive reduction to 16 pixels. Make a professionally resolved small app identity rather than a generic stock pictogram.
Constraints: no words, letters, numbers, watermark, surrounding UI, mockup, presentation board, sheet of icons, or extra decorative objects. Original design only.
Scene/backdrop: fully opaque warm-ivory square, uniform and edge to edge.
Subject: two chunky geometric connector modules interlocking into a compact ascending zigzag emblem. One module is a deep ultramarine open squared-C shape, the other is a warm-orange solid hooked shape. Their broad stepped joint communicates two website links connecting successfully, with a large clean white opening between the two forms. The whole emblem has an upward, check-like rhythm without drawing a separate check mark.
Style/medium: contemporary geometric constructivist identity, flat cut-paper shapes, carefully balanced asymmetry, crisp square corners with only slight softening. Bold and spare.
Color palette: ultramarine and warm orange on warm ivory, high contrast, no gradients.
Constraints: use only TWO large solid shapes, no classic thin chain-link outlines, no letter D, no badge, no texture, no 3D, no shadows. Broad recognizable silhouette with no tiny seams.
```
